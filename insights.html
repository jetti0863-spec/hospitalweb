Health & Medical Information


Learn more about healthcare, medical specialties, emergency care and general health awareness. Avinash Nursing Home & Hospital is committed to promoting patient awareness and encouraging people to take appropriate care of their health.



Orthopaedics


Orthopaedics focuses on conditions affecting bones, joints, muscles, ligaments and other parts of the musculoskeletal system.

Obstetrics & Gynecology


Provides medical care related to women's reproductive health, pregnancy and gynecological conditions.

Dermatology


Dermatology deals with the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the skin, hair and nails.

Neurology


Neurology focuses on conditions affecting the brain, spinal cord and nervous system.



Cardiothoracic Care


Cardiothoracic medicine involves the evaluation and management of conditions related to the heart, chest and associated structures. Patients should seek professional medical advice when experiencing symptoms or concerns related to these conditions.



24/7 Emergency Service


Avinash Nursing Home & Hospital provides 24/7 emergency service for patients who require immediate medical attention. In an emergency, seek medical assistance promptly rather than delaying treatment.



Health Awareness


  • Maintain a balanced and healthy lifestyle

  • Follow appropriate medical advice

  • Attend regular health check-ups when appropriate

  • Do not ignore persistent or unusual symptoms

  • Take prescribed medicines as directed by your doctor

  • Seek professional medical advice when needed


Important Medical Information


The information provided on this page is intended for general health awareness only. It should not be considered a substitute for professional medical diagnosis, treatment or advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding your individual medical condition.
