About Avinash Nursing Home & Hospital


Avinash Nursing Home & Hospital is committed to providing quality healthcare services with compassion, care and professionalism. Our hospital provides medical care across multiple specialties and focuses on the health, safety and well-being of our patients. With dedicated healthcare professionals and round-the-clock emergency services, we aim to provide dependable medical support to patients and their families.



Our Mission


Our mission is to provide quality and accessible healthcare while ensuring compassionate patient care, professional medical treatment and a safe healthcare environment.

Our vision is to be a trusted healthcare provider by delivering reliable medical services and patient-focused care across a range of medical specialties.
